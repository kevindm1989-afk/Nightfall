The brief, in five rules

  • One hero pet, huge. A single expressive creature fills 55–65% of the icon frame — big eyes, gold rim-light. Roblox players click faces, not scenes.
  • The rift is the backdrop. A vertical spectrum tear (green→gold→violet) behind the pet ties every asset to the brand and reads at thumbnail size.
  • Gold = value. Reserve the gold accent for loot sparkle and the pet's rim. It's the through-color across icon, UI, and thumbnails.
  • Thumbnails sell the update, not the game — "Rainbow Pets Are Here," "New Rift," "2× Luck Weekend." Refresh them every patch to re-trigger discovery.
  • Deep violet-black ground (#0B0714) everywhere — it makes the neon biome colors pop and keeps the whole store presence unmistakably one game.

Monetization

Priced to Convert, Tuned to Stay Fair

Four gamepasses and a nine-SKU product ladder — anchored by a 99-Robux Starter Pack and topped by whale-tier vaults. Every price sits on a known Robux psychological breakpoint.
